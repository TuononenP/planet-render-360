Privacy

Updated 8 Nov 2026

Planet Render 360 has no account, no advertising and no analytics. It does not ask for your location. What you look at stays on your device.

To build a place, the app asks public data services for the map, terrain, imagery and weather around it. Those services see your IP address and the place you asked for. The developer receives none of this.

What stays on your device

  • Recent places, favourites and the camera position you left each place at.
  • Your settings, such as theme, units and quality.
  • Downloaded map, terrain, imagery and building data, and scenes the app has already built, so places open faster next time.
  • Photos you save. They go to your Pictures folder, the app’s own photos folder, or the place you choose on a computer.

The app reads the motion sensor to turn the view when you use gyro look-around. It does not store or send those readings.

What leaves your device

The app contacts only the services below, and only to answer something you did. Each one also sees your IP address and the app’s name, planet-render-360.

tiles.openfreemap.org		 The tiles for the part of the map you are viewing.

overpass-api.de, z.overpass-api.de, overpass.private.coffee

Esri imagery
server.arcgisonline.com		 The satellite image tiles for the place you open.
AWS Terrain Tiles
s3.amazonaws.com		 The elevation tiles for the place you open.

CHELSA climate

Open-Meteo weather
api.open-meteo.com, archive-api.open-meteo.com		 The coordinates of the place you open, to 4 decimals.
Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons

This project’s pages
tuononenp.github.io		 A request for a small settings file at launch. It lists service addresses so they can change without an app update. GitHub hosts it and sees your IP address.

Each service keeps its own records under its own policy. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation, Esri, Amazon Web Services, Open-Meteo, Wikimedia Foundation and GitHub policies. For the other servers, check the operator’s own site.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking identifiers.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library.
  • No sale or sharing of data. The developer runs no server for the app.

Your choices

  • Remove places from History, or clear them all.
  • Clear the downloaded data under Settings → Storage.
  • Save a place for offline, then use the app with no connection and no requests.
  • Uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Photos you saved stay where you put them.

Children

The app is not aimed at children, and it collects no personal information from anyone.
