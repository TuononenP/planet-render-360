# Stockholm landmark sources: measurements for the super-realistic plan (S-0.3) - 2026-11-20

Footprints, centroids and axis bearings were measured from OpenStreetMap geometry (Overpass `out geom` on
`overpass.openstreetmap.fr`, or the OSM map API), in the format of `docs/turku-landmark-sources.md`. Reference photos
are listed per landmark in `visual-plan/refs/landmarks/<type>/README.md` (fetched with `tools/commons_refs.py`;
Wikimedia Commons, CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC0 / public domain only; JPGs are gitignored, the READMEs record URL, author
and licence). No Russian-operated host was contacted.

**Yaw convention** (checked against the models in each group): a model whose long axis runs along its +X has
footprint bearing = 90 + `yaw_deg` (mod 180); one whose long axis runs along +Z has bearing = `yaw_deg`. Heights
marked *unverified* have no second source yet; they are not changed until a photo or register confirms them.

## Hero models and churches (group A)

Footprints, centroids and axis bearings were measured directly from OpenStreetMap geometry (Overpass `out geom`, `overpass.openstreetmap.fr`, 170 m radius around each registry point). Centroid is the polygon area centroid (largest outer ring for relations); the axis is the long side of the minimum-area rotated rectangle, in degrees clockwise from north (mod 180); size is long x short of that rectangle. Registry entries live in `scripts/data/stockholm/sto_reg_hero.gd` except `riddarholmen` (`location_data.gd` `_regional_extras`) and `stockholm_city_hall` (`location_data.gd`).

Yaw convention (checked against the models): a model whose long axis runs along its +X (palace, churches, Nordic Museum, City Hall) has footprint bearing = 90 + `yaw_deg` (mod 180); a model whose long axis runs along +Z (concert hall 50 x 59, library 48 x 50) has bearing = `yaw_deg` (mod 180). Storkyrkan, Katarina, Hedvig Eleonora, Riddarholmen, the palace and the Nordic Museum all land within 1.5 degrees of OSM under this rule.

### Suggested registry corrections

1. **Hötorgsskraporna height: keep 61.0 m (registry is right, OSM's 72 is above sea level).** OSM tags all five slabs `height=72` and `building:levels=19`. Swedish Wikipedia ("Hötorgsskraporna") states "fem 61 meter (eller 72 meter över havet)" and "nitton våningar", i.e. 72 m is the roof level above sea level, 61 m is the building height; the code comment in `landmark_ss_hero_skyline.gd` (ordinance: +72 m on an +11 m base) agrees. The OSM tag is therefore the mapper using the absolute elevation. 19 storeys at 72 m would be 3.8 m per storey, implausible for these offices; 61 m gives 3.2 m. No registry change. Optional: slab width 39 m in `_hotorg_slab` is smaller than the OSM slab (43.4-44.5 m x 14.1-15.3 m); 43.5 x 14.5 would match.
2. **Hötorgsskraporna position and yaw: no change.** The registry centre is 0.95 m from the middle slab and the four `clear_offsets_m` points are within 0.8-2.5 m of the OSM slab centres; the slab long axis (64.9-65.6) agrees with yaw -25.2 + 90 = 64.8.
3. **`grand_hotel_stockholm` (needs fixing).** Centroid is 26.9 m off. Propose lat 59.329842, lon 18.075628, yaw -51.7 (so the 78 m x-long block follows the 38.3 deg ranges; the registry -38 has the wrong sign), keep height 30.0 flagged unverified. Consider that the real footprint is an 80 x 79 m courtyard plan: a single 78 x 26 slab will not cover it.
4. **`nationalmuseum_stockholm` (marginal).** Centroid is 8.2 m off. Propose lat 59.328481, lon 18.078183. Yaw -38.0 is fine (OSM -39.1); -39.1 would be tighter. Height 33.5 matches OSM.
5. **`soder_torn` yaw.** Registry has no `yaw_deg` (0); the mapped octagon is rotated about 13 deg. Propose `yaw_deg` -13.3 (facets then sit at about 32 deg mod 45 as in OSM). Position and 86 m height are exact. Flagged as medium confidence because it relies on the yaw convention above and on the model's facet orientation (frustum offset PI/8, facing 0, 45, 90 deg).

## Tier C: outlying landmarks and the reach analysis (S-3, 2026-11-20)

### What reaches from the Monteliusvägen preset (59.3207, 18.0604)

Three limits decide it, all read from the code:

- **Landmark build limit, 9 km.** `TerrainBuilder.LANDMARK_MAX_M = 9000` skips any candidate farther than 9 km from the world centre, whatever its registry `radius_m`. (The 12 km figure is the far-city layer, `FarCityFetcher.RADIUS_M`, which draws OSM towers and landuse massing, not hand-built models.)
- **Candidate selection.** `LocationData.registry_extras` offers a landmark only when the viewpoint lies within its `radius_m`. A radius that reaches the preset from beyond 9 km would take a candidate slot and then never be built.
- **Ultra budget.** Stockholm Ultra builds 52 candidates, 48 of them non-bridge icons (`ultra_registry_limit` 48, `ultra_total_limit` 52): `skyline_priority` items first, then nearest first. Measured before Tier C: 62 extras were in range (13 bridges, 49 icons), the 49th icon (Bromma airport, 7.6 km) was the one dropped. Every Tier B type adds to that, so a low Tier C landmark 3 to 7 km out is not built at the preset on Ultra; only a `skyline_priority` one is. A landmark does show whenever the scene centre moves toward it (streaming) or on presets where the nearer icons leave room.
- Beyond 4.2 km a built landmark is stripped to its octahedral impostor (`LandmarkImpostor.STRIP_REAL_M`), so the far ones stay cheap.

### Decisions

- `strawberry_arena` and `nya_karolinska` carry `skyline_priority`: their tall element (the 94 m hotel; the 60 m Carolina Tower) stands above the Vasastan roofs from Monteliusvägen (vertical angle about 0.6 degrees against 0.3 for the roofline) and is 40 m or more. The arena bowl itself is 33 m and would not qualify alone, so the model keeps the hotel inside the same entry.
- `kth_main`, `stockholm_university` and `naturhistoriska_riksmuseet` are low (25 to 50 m) and carry no priority: they appear from nearer scene centres and in a streamed world, not in the Ultra preset budget.
- `radius_m` is the distance to the preset plus a margin (7,000 / 5,800 / 3,500 / 5,200 / 4,000 m), never the 9 km limit.
