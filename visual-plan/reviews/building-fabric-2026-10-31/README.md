# Building fabric and regional styles — 2026-10-31

High preset, 15:00, 1280×800, windowed app. Captured after the street-fabric,
relief and city-style work. The before state for Paris is
`visual-plan/best/03b-view360-desktop.png`: separate beige boxes with hipped
roofs, all shaped alike.

| Capture | What to look for |
|---|---|
| `paris_y0`, `paris_y314`, `paris_y-157` | Continuous Haussmann street walls: party walls shared, mansards running along the street, dormers on the window axes, 2nd/5th-floor iron balconies, red café awnings, a modern glass office (`paris_modern`) among the stone. |
| `paris_down360` | Roofscape from above: chimney stacks with pots on party walls, courtyard blocks, cornices. |
| `london_y0`, `london_y157` | Brick and stucco terraces, rows of party-wall chimney stacks, parapets, mansion blocks and council estates. |
| `amsterdam_y314` | Dark brick canal rows with step and neck gables facing the street. |
| `nyc_y0` | East Village red-brick walk-ups with fire escapes and shop awnings along Tompkins Square Park. |
| `tokyo_y0` | Dense small concrete and tile blocks with balconies and vertical lit signs. |

Known residue: the big multi-part Hôtel de Ville roof still shows a few
steep facets from the chamfered (inset) roof over its concave parts. Party
gables are limited to parcel-like rings, so the shards seen in the first
pass are gone.
