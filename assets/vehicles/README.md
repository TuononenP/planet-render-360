# Parked vehicle models

“Generic passenger car pack” by [Comrade1280](https://sketchfab.com/comrade1280),
[original asset](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/generic-passenger-car-pack-20f9af9b8a404d5cb022ac6fe87f21f5),
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The FBX files and
textures were obtained from the [TUM-VT redistribution](https://github.com/TUM-VT/Sumonity-PassengerCars/tree/92be5975162ff9348c96d76dd6077321dce013dc).

Modified for Planet Render 360: five models were combined with their wheels,
rescaled, reoriented and exported as GLB. Textures were resized, and the body
textures were adapted to include a paint mask for per-instance color. The
source's attribution notice is preserved in LICENSE.txt.
